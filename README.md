# Hifz Vision

**A Quran app for Android to read, listen and memorize.** It works for everyone, and it's built to be the most accessible Quran app for blind and visually impaired people using TalkBack: every screen, button and setting works fully with a screen reader.

## [Download the latest version (APK)](https://github.com/mohammedtahadev/hifz_vision-releases/raw/main/apk/hifz_vision-latest.apk)

Free, no ads, no account needed. Android 7.0 or newer.

## What you can do

- **Read** the whole Quran in clear Arabic script, with text you can make bigger or smaller. It works without internet.
- **Listen** to about twenty reciters, ayah by ayah, including eleven imams of the Haramain.
- **Repeat** one ayah, a ruku or a whole surah as many times as you like, with an optional pause to recite it yourself.
- **Move through a recitation** with sliders by time, by ayah, by ruku or by quarter of a para. With TalkBack you just swipe up or down, and it tells you where you are, like "Ruku 37" or "Al-Baqarah, Ayah 255".
- **Memorize**: keep a list of the ayat and rukus you are learning. Choose one and it plays on repeat, and the app counts how often you've practised each one.
- **Download** a surah, a para or the whole Quran and listen with no internet.
- **Change the speed** from 0.25 to 2 times, and set a sleep timer.
- **Bookmarks**, the Hijri date, and optional Google sign-in to keep your bookmarks and progress when you change phones.

Coming in the next update: the Quran in your own language (about 120 translations), prayer times, and Hifzy, a helper you can ask how to use any part of the app.

## How to install

1. Open the download link above on your Android phone.
2. When the download finishes, open the file.
3. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps ("Allow from this source"), then go back and choose Install.

The app tells you when a new version is ready and installs it when you choose Update. You don't need to come back here.
