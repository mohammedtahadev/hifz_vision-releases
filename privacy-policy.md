# hifz_vision Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 21 October 2026

hifz_vision is a free Quran reading, listening and memorisation app for Android, made to work well with screen readers. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: **hifzvision@gmail.com**

## The short version

- You can use the whole app **without an account**. The app still tells our server, each time it opens, which phone model and app version it runs on, so we can count how many people use it and stop misuse (see "Your phone and the app").
- If you **sign in with Google**, your profile, bookmarks and last reading and listening place are saved online, so you can get them back on a new phone.
- We do **not** sell your information, show ads or use advertising trackers.
- You can **delete your account and online data** at any time from inside the app.
- **Hifzy**, the in-app helper, answers from the app's own guide without internet. When you are online, the question you type is sent to our server so Hifzy can answer it in its own words, and the question is kept for 30 days (see below).
- **Prayer times** use the city you type, or your approximate location if you choose "Use my location". Your location is used only to work out prayer times and is not saved online.

## Information saved online when you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in, the app saves the following to our online database:

| Information | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Google account's email address and name | To know which record is yours, and to contact you about your account if needed |
| The name, bio and country you entered in the app | To bring your profile back on a new phone |
| Your gender, only if you choose one (it is optional, and "Prefer not to say" is an option) | To understand who uses the app, as a count of how many users are male or female |
| Your bookmarked ayat | To bring your bookmarks back on a new phone |
| Your Memorize list, with how many times and when you practised each ayah or ruku | To bring your practice list back on a new phone |
| The last surah or para you read and listened to | To let you continue where you left off |
| The app version and the last time the app saved your data | To help us fix problems and understand which versions are still in use |

We do not collect your password. Google handles sign-in, and we only receive your email address, your name and an account identifier.

## Who can see it

- **You** can see and change your own information in the app.
- **The hifz_vision team** can view the online records to run and improve the app, and to answer support requests.
- **Google (Firebase)** stores the information for us as our service provider. It is kept in Google's data centre in Mumbai, India. Google processes it under its own terms and privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not share, sell or rent your information to anyone else. We will only disclose it if the law requires us to.

## Your phone and the app

Each time the app opens with internet, it tells our server (server.swarupbaral.in) that it is being used, with or without an account. It sends:

- your phone's Android ID, a number Android gives each app on each phone, which stays the same if the app is reinstalled;
- the phone's maker and model, its Android version, and the app version;
- the country from your profile, or your phone's region, and your phone's language;
- your Google account's email address, only if you signed in to the app.

We use this to count how many people use the app and on which phones and versions, and to stop the app on a phone that misuses it: the team can block a phone, and the app then stops working on it. The server answers only whether the phone is blocked. Only the hifz_vision team can see this information, in an admin app protected by a name and PIN for each team member.

About once an hour, and when it opens, the app also asks our server for messages from the hifz_vision team, sending the same Android ID, and shows any as a notification. Our server notes which phones received each message.

A phone's record is deleted a year after the app was last used on it. A blocked phone's record is kept while it is blocked.

## Hifzy, the in-app helper

Hifzy answers questions about using the app. Without internet, it answers on your phone from the app's own guide, and nothing is sent anywhere.

When you are online, Hifzy first asks our server (server.swarupbaral.in), so it can answer in its own words. For that, the app sends:

- the question you typed, and your last few questions and answers to Hifzy, so a follow-up question makes sense;
- a random number made once for your copy of the app, used only to limit how many questions can be asked each day. It is not linked to your name, your Google account or your phone.

Our server passes the question to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to write the answer, and sends the answer back to your phone. Anthropic processes it as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to use the question to train its models. Anthropic's privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Our server keeps each question for 30 days, so the team can see what people ask and make the app and Hifzy's answers better: the question, when it was asked, its language, and the random number above. It does not keep the answers, and it never keeps your IP address with a question. It also keeps daily counts of questions (per random number and per IP address, to stop misuse) and the size of each answer, to track the cost. Questions older than 30 days are deleted. Please do not type personal information into Hifzy.

## Prayer times and your location

To show prayer times, the app needs to know your city. You can type a city and country, or choose "Use my location". If you choose your location, Android asks for your approximate location first, and you can say no and type a city instead.

The city, or your approximate position, is sent to Aladhan (api.aladhan.com), a free prayer times service, to get the month's times. Android's own location service turns your position into a place name. The place you choose is saved on your phone only, so the times work offline. It is never added to your online profile.

## Recite and check

Recite and check listens to you recite through the microphone, only while you have pressed Start. Your recitation is checked on your phone by a speech recognition model, and is not sent to us or anyone else. The last recitation is kept on your phone only, so that you can send it to us yourself with "Share last recording" if you want to report a problem. The model is downloaded once from GitHub.

## Other services the app connects to

- **Quran recitation audio** is streamed from cdn.islamic.network, everyayah.com, cdn.mp3quran.net and media.way2quran.com, depending on the reciter. As with any website, these services receive your device's IP address when audio is played.
- **Quran translations** are downloaded once from Al Quran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) when you choose one.
- **App updates** and the Recite and check model are downloaded from GitHub (github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com), which likewise receives your device's IP address.

These requests do not include your profile, bookmarks or Memorize list.

## How long we keep it

We keep your online information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your online record is deleted immediately, and your sign-in account is removed from our project.

## Your choices

- **Use the app without signing in.** Your profile, bookmarks and Memorize list are then never saved online; only the phone details described under "Your phone and the app" are.
- **Use Hifzy offline.** With no internet, Hifzy's answers come from your phone and nothing is sent.
- **Type your city** for prayer times instead of sharing your location.
- **Sign out** at any time in You → Profile → Account. What was already saved online stays there until you delete it.
- **Delete your account and online data** in You → Profile → Account → Delete my account and online data.
- **Ask us.** You can also email hifzvision@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted.

## Children

hifz_vision is used by people of all ages who are learning the Quran. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without a parent's or guardian's involvement. Children can use the app fully without signing in. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has signed in without your consent, email us and we will delete the account.

## Security

Information sent between the app and our database or server is encrypted in transit. Our database rules allow each account to read and change only its own record. The admin app needs each team member's own name and PIN, and locks a name after five wrong PINs.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If a change affects what is collected, we will describe it in the app's update notes.
